# Code of conduct

We welcome contributors regardless of background or experience. Be respectful,
give specific and constructive feedback, and discuss the work rather than attack
the person. Respect privacy and attribute others' work.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, and publishing
someone's private information without permission are not acceptable in issues,
pull requests, discussions, or other project spaces.

Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting form to request confidential contact
with the maintainers. If that form is unavailable, open a minimal public issue
asking for a private channel and include no incident details or personal data.
Maintainers will protect the reporter's privacy as far as practical and may
remove content, warn participants, or restrict participation. Maintainers are
subject to the same expectations. Decisions should be proportionate and explain
the behavior that needs to change.
